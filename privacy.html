Privacy policy

What Empire Sort stores, and what it never sends.

Effective date: November 7, 2026

Summary

Empire Sort does not ask for your name, email, contacts, location, or photos, and it does not need an account. Your game progress is stored only on your iPhone, and nothing about how you play is sent to me. If you choose to use Game Center, your leaderboard scores and achievements are sent to Apple, as described below.

Information stored on your iPhone

To make the game work, Empire Sort saves the following on your device only:

  • Your progress: rank, wins, mastery, achievements, collection discoveries, and daily and weekly results
  • Your settings, such as text size, sound, difficulty, and reminder time
  • Gameplay statistics, such as which cards you have seen and misplaced
  • A saved copy of a game in progress

This information is not sent to me or anyone else. Settings → Reset all progress deletes your progress, stats, and saved games, and deleting the app removes everything the game has stored.

Daily Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, the reminder is scheduled on your iPhone by iOS. No information is sent to me. You can turn it off in the game’s Settings or in your iPhone’s Settings → Notifications.

Game Center

Empire Sort can use Apple’s Game Center for leaderboards and achievements. This is optional: you can switch it off at any time in the game’s Settings, under Game Center, and also in your iPhone’s Settings.

When Game Center is on and you are signed in to it, the game sends Apple your moves on the weekly puzzle, your longest Daily streak and the medals you earn, so they can appear on the leaderboards and in your achievements. Apple handles this under its own privacy policy and your Game Center settings, and it may show your Game Center name and score to other players on a leaderboard. I do not receive your name, your Apple ID, your Game Center ID or your scores, and I do not run a server that stores them.

If you turn Game Center off or sign out, the game stops sending anything. Your progress in the game stays on your iPhone and does not depend on Game Center. For more, see Apple’s privacy information for Game Center.

Sharing you choose to do

When you tap Share (for a result, a fact, or a friend challenge), the game creates text or an image and hands it to your iPhone’s share sheet. You choose where it goes. A challenge code contains only the cards in that deal, the move limit, and your move count. It contains no personal information.

Exporting your data

Settings → Export my play data creates a copy of your gameplay statistics that you can send to someone yourself. It is never sent automatically.

The game also has a Share anonymous play data switch. At the moment it sends nothing, because no online service is connected. If that ever changes, this policy will be updated before anything is sent.

Links to other websites

Learn more notes link to outside sources such as UNESCO, Britannica, the Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, The Met, and the Fondation Napoléon. Those links open in Safari, and those websites have their own privacy policies.

Children

Empire Sort is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated, and significant changes will be noted in the app’s update notes.
