Support

Help with Empire Sort, the history sorting puzzle for iPhone.

Contact

Email empiresortsupport@gmail.com. I usually reply within a few days.

When reporting a problem, please include your iPhone model, your iOS version, and what happened. To report a mistake on a card, include the card’s name and its empire, and a source if you have one.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play?
Move each gold category card into a display case, then move every card that belongs to that empire onto it. Tap any card to read about it. A full tutorial is under How to play → Play the tutorial.
Why did my game end?
Every game has a move limit set just above the fastest route the computer can find. Drawing, undoing, hints, and wrong placements all use moves. You can choose a more relaxed limit in Settings → Difficulty, or play Zen mode with no limit.
How do I unlock more empires and harder cards?
Win games to rise in rank, which unlocks more empires. Sorting an empire with no wrong placements earns mastery, which unlocks that empire’s harder cards.
How does a friend challenge work?
After a free-play game, tap Challenge a friend and send the code. Your friend taps Friend’s challenge on the home screen and pastes it to play the same deal.
How do I turn the daily reminder on or off?
In Settings → Daily reminder. You can also turn off notifications for Empire Sort in your iPhone’s Settings → Notifications.
Will I lose my progress if I change phones?
Right now, progress is stored only on your iPhone, so it doesn’t move to a new phone.
How do I delete my data?
Settings → Reset all progress deletes your progress, stats, and saved games. Deleting the app removes everything the game has stored.
I found a mistake on a card.
Thank you! Email me with the card’s name and empire, and a source if you have one.
